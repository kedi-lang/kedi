# Delivery policy

## Delayed parcels

Ask for the order number and the carrier's latest tracking update. Support can
open a carrier investigation once a parcel is at least three business days
past its estimated delivery date. An estimated date is not a guarantee.

## Refund eligibility

A delayed parcel is not automatically eligible for a refund. If the carrier
confirms loss, a support specialist can offer a replacement or refund after
verifying the order. Never state that either action has already happened
unless an order-system record confirms it.

## Response times

Carrier investigations usually receive a first update within two business
days. This is an estimate for an update, not a promised delivery or refund date.
